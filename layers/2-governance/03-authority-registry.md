## Registered Authorities

:::rcos{clauses="4.3.1, 4.3.2, 4.3.3, 4.3.4, 4.3.5"}
:::

:::rationale{title="Why write every authority down?"}
Authority that is not explicitly registered gets filled in by default — by whoever has been around longest, speaks loudest, or controls the keys. A single registry that names every role, bounds its scope, caps its limits, and ties its basis to a delegation act makes unauthorized action detectable and prevents authority from being derived from charisma, seniority, or ownership.
:::

| Role / Body | Scope of authority | Limits | Term / Duration | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Active Members (collective) | All Strategic and Constitutional decisions as defined in the Decision Matrix | Cannot act outside the Decision Matrix; cannot override invariants; no individual Active Member has unilateral authority | Ongoing — any Active Member in good standing | Membership state (Layer 1) |
| Communal Finance / Accountant | Treasury administration: accounting ledger management, treasury records, spending compliance | Spending limit per Treasury Ruleset (currently $100 USD); no unilateral spending authority; cannot override Active Member votes | Ongoing — see Role Registry (Layer 5) | Delegated by Active Members via the governance process |
| Soc. Media Manager | Communications: documenting and promoting Fruit Haven online, publishing and removing content | Within accounts regulations; cannot make partnership or brand-use decisions without a Strategic vote; cannot override Active Member votes | Ongoing — see Role Registry (Layer 5) | Delegated by Active Members via the governance process |
| Meetings Organizer / Facilitator | Organizing and facilitating community meetings; filing proposal records | Limited to weekly community meetings for now; cannot override Active Member votes | Ongoing — see Role Registry (Layer 5) | Delegated by Active Members via the governance process |
| C.A. (Community Area) Manager | Platform and facility operations: routine repair, maintenance, and security of the community area | Cannot make structural changes without a Strategic vote; cannot override Active Member votes | Ongoing — see Role Registry (Layer 5) | Delegated by Active Members via the governance process |

> _<Reserved — authority over digital platforms, websites, and hosted services (e.g. fruithaven.land) is not yet named in any ratified bylaws or doctrine; see scope-declaration item 4. Pending future ratification.>_

> **Note:** Operational roles are filled per the Role Registry (Layer 5); during the early phase of the community several roles may be held by the same member. Role definitions, responsibilities, and handover requirements are fully specified in the Role Registry (Layer 5).

> **Conflict-of-interest recusal:** An operational role holder may not exercise their delegated authority in any decision where they have a direct personal interest (e.g. the Meetings Organizer/Facilitator may not process their own admission, exit, or accountability check; the Communal Finance/Accountant may not approve their own reimbursement). In such cases, the decision is escalated to Active Members via the governance process.

> Authority must not be derived from charisma, seniority, ownership, or informal influence.  
> Temporary or emergency authority must be explicitly defined, time-bounded, and subject to review.
