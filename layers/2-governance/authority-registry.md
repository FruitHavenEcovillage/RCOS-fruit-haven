# Authority Registry

- **Layer:** 2 — Governance & Decision Logic
- **Status:** Stub — not yet adopted
- **RCOS reference:** §4.3, §4.7

> Lists all roles, circles, or bodies that hold decision-making authority, along with their explicit scope, limits, and term.

---

## Registered Authorities

| Role / Body | Scope of authority | Limits | Term / Duration | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full Members (collective) | All Strategic and Constitutional decisions as defined in the Decision Matrix | Cannot act outside the Decision Matrix; cannot override invariants; no individual Full Member has unilateral authority | Ongoing — any Full Member in good standing | Membership state (Layer 1) |
| Admin | All Operational decisions within delegated limits; managing platforms, membership processing, RCOS artifact maintenance, community support activities | Spending limit: €0 (current); cannot make Strategic or Constitutional decisions unilaterally; cannot override Full Member votes | Ongoing — role defined in Role Registry (Layer 5) | Delegated by Full Members via the governance process |

> **Note:** "Admin" is currently a placeholder for a set of distinct operational roles (e.g. Social Media Steward, Infrastructure Steward, Finance Steward, Membership Admin) that will be formally defined in the Role Registry (Layer 5). Once Layer 5 is complete, this registry and the Decision Matrix will be updated to reference the specific roles.

> Authority must not be derived from charisma, seniority, ownership, or informal influence.  
> Temporary or emergency authority must be explicitly defined, time-bounded, and subject to review.
