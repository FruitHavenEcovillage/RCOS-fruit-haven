# Role Registry

- **Layer:** 5 — Operations & Coordination
- **Status:** Present
- **RCOS reference:** §7.1, §7.6

---

## Overview

:::rcos{clauses="7.1.1, 7.1.2, 7.1.4, 7.7.1"}
:::

:::rationale{title="Why require every responsibility to have a named role?"}
Ongoing responsibilities without explicit roles become invisible labor — done by whoever notices, resented silently, and impossible to hand over. Making every ongoing responsibility a named, accountable role is what prevents the community from running on the unpaid goodwill of a few members.
:::

This registry defines all recognized roles within Fruit Haven. Every role has a defined purpose, authority, limit, and term length.

> **"In good standing"** means a Full Member who has met their participation expectations in the last 6 months and is not currently subject to an active accountability process or conflict review under Layer 4.

## Defined Roles

| Role | Purpose | Authority | Limits | Term Length (min/max) | Selection method |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Communal Finance/Accountant** (min 2) | Continuous documentation of finances. Cash holding / communal treasury management. | Approve expenses and investments; view balance & transactions of communal treasury; can grant spendings below $100 USD for other managers. | None; can operate within defined purpose. Any spending above $100 USD needs a proposal and voting process. | min: 3 months<br />max: 12 months (renewable) | Selected from members based on skills or motivation |
| **Financial Bookkeeper** | Data entry: Income / expenses tracking. | None. | None; can operate within defined purpose. | min: 3 months<br />max: 12 months (renewable) | Selected from members based on skills or motivation |
| **C.A. (Community Area) Manager** (1) | Acts like "Facility manager". General repair and maintenance (e.g. Water & Electricity) and security around the community area (e.g. Cameras). | Decide over regular quiet hours; when to do a cleaning; closing hours/areas for guests & volunteers; create proposals for bigger investments. | Within defined scope. | min: 3 months<br />max: 12 months (renewable) | Selected from members based on skills or motivation |
| **Hospitality Manager** (1) | Coordinates the arrival of guests; responsible for guests/volunteers hospitality; availability on call for questions; prevents double-booking of rooms; accepts payments and forwards them to the accountant. | Refuse and relocate guests. | Needs approval from owner for private homestead lots for relocation. | min: 3 months<br />max: 12 months (renewable) | Selected from members based on skills or motivation |
| **Nursery Manager** (1) | Keeping plants alive; organization: labeling, propagating, pruning; preparing plants for sale; forwarding sales income to accountant. | Stocking, inventory organization, which plants to hold/propagate. | Within defined scope. | min: 3 months<br />max: 12 months (renewable) | Selected from members based on skills or motivation |
| **Project Manager** (1 per project) | Direct specific projects (e.g. fixing a section of road, water system or CA building). Project Owner; Project planning; Supervision of work process. | To the specific project. | Limited to the specific project scope. | min: 3 months (or project length)<br />max: 12 months (renewable) | Selected from members based on skills or motivation |
| **Volunteer Coordinator** (1) | Direct workshops and projects, assign work, decide details for volunteers. | Maintenance calls and decisions, leading. | Within business hours. | min: 3 months<br />max: 12 months (renewable) | Selected from members based on skills or motivation |
| **Soc. Media Manager** (1) | Document, promote Fruit Haven online. | Publishing, removing, select content. | Within accounts regulations. | min: 3 months<br />max: 12 months (renewable) | Selected from members based on skills or motivation |
| **Meetings Organizer/ Facilitator** (1) | Organization and facilitation of meetings. | Organize, publicize, facilitate or delegate a facilitator for weekly community meetings. | For now, only focusing on weekly community meetings. | min: 1 month<br />max: 12 months (renewable) | Selected from members based on skills or motivation |
| **Workshop Manager** (1) | Manage inventory of hardware and power tools. | Over workshop hardware and tools. | Within defined scope. | min: 3 months<br />max: 12 months (renewable) | Selected from members based on skills or motivation |
| **Volunteer** | Work as assistant/fellowship/internship to above mentioned roles. | Volunteers have no authority. Decisions need to be made by manager/coordinator. | Suggested contribution 20h/week. Coordinator decides details. | No minimum/maximum length | Consent based decision between full members via application |
| **Guest** | Can take over Volunteer work. | Guests have no authority. Decisions need to be made by manager/coordinator. | No contribution hours per week. | No minimum/maximum length | Consent based decision between Active Members (for Community Housing) |
