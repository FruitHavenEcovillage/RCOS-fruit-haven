## Meeting Type: Conflict Handling

- **Purpose:** Facilitate resolution of a raised conflict at a defined ladder step; this template covers facilitated sessions (Ladder Steps 2–4)
- **Decision scope:** Produces repair plans, agreements, or escalation decisions; binding outcomes require the authority defined in the Conflict Resolution Ladder and Decision Matrix
- **Required participants:** Involved parties; assigned facilitator
- **Optional participants:** [To be defined — e.g. support person, observer]
- **Cadence:** As needed; triggered by conflict intake, not scheduled
- **Duration limit:** [To be defined]
- **Facilitation:** Selected per Conflict Resolution Ladder facilitator selection rules — not by the parties themselves

> **Privacy note:** Records from this meeting type are subject to the privacy and information access boundaries defined in the Conflict Resolution Ladder. They are not published to the general member record by default.

---

## Agenda Structure

1. **Opening and ground rules** — facilitator confirms consent to process, confidentiality boundaries, and anti-retaliation protections
2. **Each party states their perspective** (uninterrupted, time-boxed)
3. **Shared understanding check** — facilitator reflects back; parties confirm or correct
4. **Needs and requests** — what each party needs for resolution
5. **Repair or agreement options** — generate options without committing
6. **Decision or next step** — agree on repair plan, escalation, or adjournment with timeline
7. **Documentation** — facilitator records outcome per privacy rules; parties confirm record
