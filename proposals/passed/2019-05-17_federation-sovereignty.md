## Summary

Establishes the sovereignty and federation rules for Fruit Haven community areas, as set out in the [Fruit Haven Community Sovereignty Doctrine](/source-documents/FH_Community_Sovereignty_Doctrine.pdf). It outlines community autonomy, access rules between communities, and the formation of new sovereign communities.

- \[x\] Permanent rule change
- \[ \] Time-bounded experiment

## Rationale

To explicitly define the relationships between different Fruit Haven communities, properties, and the rules governing self-governance and access across the federation.

## Risks and Mitigations

Risk: Communities may fracture or establish conflicting rules. Mitigation: The charter establishes clear supremacy guidelines, maintaining that the core charter takes precedence where [community bylaws](/source-documents/FH1_Community_Bylaws.pdf) conflict.

## Transition and Migration Plan

No migration required.

## Rollback Plan

Reversion to prior unstructured property relations through a new consensus vote.
