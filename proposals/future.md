# Future Proposals

Further ideas that will be defined in future decisions:

---

## High Priority (Core governance & operations)

**Membership States**
Defines the membership states: Applicant, Trial Member, Active Member, and Exited Member / External. Includes their rights, obligations, and transition triggers.

**Transitional Membership Pathway for Current Owners**
Provides a transitional pathway for current property owners to be granted Active Member status for a 1-year period, during which they must fulfill membership requirements.

**Responsibilities of Owners / Trustees**
Clarifies the responsibilities of Owners / Trustees as legal stewards of the property, distinct from general membership.

**Roles Explicitness**
Defines clear roles, purposes, authorities, limits, and term lengths for various community positions (Communal Finance, C.A. Manager, etc.).

---

## Medium Priority (Membership structure & expectations)

**Definition of Participation in Community Governance**
Defines participation for Active Members as a combination of Responsiveness, Contribution, Attendance, and Accountability.

---

## Lower Priority (Conflict & process refinement)

**Conflict Resolution Updates**
Updates the conflict resolution ladder with explicit conflict classes and an Emergency Safety Protocol.

**FH Federation**
Outlines community autonomy, inter-community access, and sovereignty requirements for new Fruit Haven communities.

---

## Further Definition Ideas

Items raised alongside the draft proposals above that are not yet drafted as proposals — left for future definition.

### High Priority (Core governance & operations)
- Review and update Community Bylaws
- Define limits and responsibilities of Communal Finance / Accountant (budgeting, spending limits, voting, and proposal processes)
- Define minimum and maximum term lengths for managing/coordinating roles (incentives and renewal conditions)
- Define term length (min + max) for Financial Bookkeeper
- Define limits on the number of roles an individual can hold
- Define Exit Protocol for Active Members

### Medium Priority (Membership structure & expectations)
- Define Volunteer Application Process
- Add a role for infographics and other materials to explain the governance layer (decisions, proposals, membership states, roles, exit-paths, etc.)
- Define general volunteering requirements (minimum hours per week)
- Define volunteering work tracking and logging system
- Clarify whether volunteers can compensate missing hours financially
- Define Trial Member participation expectations and process (e.g. accumulate at least 3 non-consecutive months of in-person or represented presence; have stayed at least 6 months within the past 10 years; physical and financial contribution expectations; application/processing fee, e.g. $10)
- Define criteria and potential reduction of Trial Membership period

### Lower Priority (Financial contributions & roles refinement)
- Define Membership fees for Trial Members transitioning to Active Members
- Define yearly contributions for Active Members (non-owners) — currently none; determine if contributions should be introduced
- Define Active Member work contribution expectations
- Provide detailed definition of responsibilities and purpose of C.A. (Community Area) Manager
