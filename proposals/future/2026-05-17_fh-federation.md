# Proposal: FH Federation

- **Status:** Draft
- **Submitted:** 2026-05-17
- **Submitted by:** Working Group
- **Decision type:** Strategic
- **Authorized decision path:** Full Members Vote

---

## Summary
Outlines community autonomy, inter-community access, and the sovereignty requirements for new Fruit Haven communities — clarifying that a community area becomes sovereign once it has at least one resident Fruit Haven member and two additional residents (or three residents total including at least one Fruit Haven member) plus written bylaws, after which it gains autonomy over residency, membership, and daily operations.
